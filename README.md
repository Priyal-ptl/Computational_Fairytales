# The Hidden Mathematics of Storytelling

A computational look at the Grimm Brothers’ fairy tales: whether recurring narrative patterns (motifs like royalty, magic, family, animals) show up across stories the way folklorist Vladimir Propp described.

**Notebook:** [grimms-fairytales.ipynb](grimms-fairytales.ipynb)  
**Dataset:** [grimms_fairytales.csv](grimms_fairytales.csv) (source: Kaggle)

## What it explores

- Cleaning and preparing the tale texts (lowercase, punctuation, stopwords)
- Grouping related words into narrative motifs
- How often each motif appears across the corpus
- Treating motif presence as a probability (Bernoulli) across stories

Made as part of a Data Science for Creative Industries course.
